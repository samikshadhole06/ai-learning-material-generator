# 📖 Quick User Guide - AI Learning Material Generator

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Access Your App
Visit your Streamlit Cloud app URL (wait 2-3 minutes after deployment)

### 2. Choose Your Learning Preferences (Sidebar)

#### **Learning Level**
- **Beginner**: Simple explanations, basic concepts, foundational knowledge
- **Intermediate**: Balanced technical content, practical applications
- **Advanced**: Complex theory, in-depth analysis, advanced concepts

#### **Study Mode**
- **Quick Revision**: Concise bullet points, key facts, memory aids
- **Exam Preparation**: Exam-focused, sample questions, tips & tricks
- **Detailed Study**: Comprehensive explanations, extended examples

### 3. Upload Your Material
- Click the file uploader
- Select a PDF document
- Wait for processing (shows metrics after completion)

### 4. Generate Smart Notes
1. Go to **"📝 Smart Notes"** tab
2. Click **"GENERATE SMART NOTES"** button
3. Wait for AI to create your personalized notes
4. Review the generated content with:
   - Structured sections
   - Visual diagrams and flowcharts
   - Key insights and tips
   - Level-appropriate explanations

### 5. Download Your Notes
Choose your preferred format:
- **📥 Markdown**: For editing and note-taking apps
- **📥 PDF**: For printing and sharing

### 6. Generate Practice Quiz
1. Go to **"🧠 Quiz Generator"** tab
2. Select difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard)
3. Choose number of questions (3-10)
4. Click **"GENERATE QUIZ"**
5. Download as needed

## 🎯 Best Practices

### For Best Results:
1. **Upload clear, well-formatted PDFs**
2. **Choose appropriate learning level** for your knowledge
3. **Select study mode** based on your goal
4. **Review the keywords** to ensure proper extraction
5. **Regenerate if needed** with different settings

### Optimal PDF Types:
- ✅ Textbooks and academic papers
- ✅ Lecture notes and presentations
- ✅ Research articles
- ✅ Technical documentation
- ❌ Image-heavy PDFs (text must be extractable)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. **Experiment with Combinations**
Try different level + mode combinations:
- Beginner + Quick Revision = Simplified bullet points
- Advanced + Detailed Study = Comprehensive analysis
- Intermediate + Exam Prep = Balanced exam focus

### 2. **Use Both Downloads**
- **Markdown**: Edit, add your own notes, use in Obsidian/Notion
- **PDF**: Print, annotate, share with classmates

### 3. **Leverage Visual Elements**
- Look for ASCII diagrams to understand processes
- Follow flowcharts for step-by-step understanding
- Use tables for quick comparisons

### 4. **Multiple PDFs**
- Upload different chapters separately for focused notes
- Compare notes from different sources
- Build a comprehensive study library

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Issue: No text extracted
- **Solution**: PDF might be image-based, use OCR tool first

### Issue: Keywords seem off
- **Solution**: PDF quality issue, try a different source

### Issue: Notes too simple/complex
- **Solution**: Adjust learning level in sidebar

### Issue: PDF generation fails
- **Solution**: Use Markdown download instead
